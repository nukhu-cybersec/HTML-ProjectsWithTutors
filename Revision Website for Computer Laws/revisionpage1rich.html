Data Protection & Client Confidentiality

Comprehensive guidelines on handling sensitive client information and GDPR compliance

📖 Policy Overview As a private investigations firm, we handle highly sensitive personal data daily. All employees must comply with UK GDPR (Data Protection Act 2018) and maintain the highest standards of confidentiality. Non-compliance can result in fines up to £17.5 million or 4% of annual turnover.

💡 Key Fact: The UK ICO reported over 2,500 data breach notifications in 2024. Proper training and procedures are essential to avoid becoming a statistic.
🎯 What is Personal Data?
  • Basic identifiers: Names, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses
  • Sensitive data: Financial information, health records, criminal records
  • Digital identifiers: IP addresses, device IDs, location data
  • Biometric data: Photos, fingerprints, voice recordings
  • Online identifiers: Social media profiles, usernames, cookies
🔐 Seven Principles of Data Protection
Principle What It Means
1. Lawfulness Must have legal basis (consent, contract, legal obligation, vital interests, public task, or legitimate interests)
2. Purpose Limitation Only collect data for specific, explicit purposes - no "just in case"
3. Data Minimisation Collect only what's necessary for the investigation
4. Accuracy Keep records up-to-date and correct errors immediately
5. Storage Limitation Delete data once it's no longer needed (standard: 7 years)
6. Security Protect against unauthorized access, loss, or damage
7. Accountability Demonstrate compliance through documentation
✅ Mandatory Security Requirements
  • All digital files encrypted with AES-256 minimum standard
  • Physical files locked in secure cabinets with restricted key access
  • USB drives and portable storage must be encrypted and registered
  • Case files accessed only on need-to-know basis - documented each time
  • Remote access only via company VPN with 2FA authentication
  • Shred all physical documents using cross-cut shredders (minimum DIN P-4)
  • Clean desk policy enforced - no sensitive materials visible when unattended
⚠️ Common Data Breach Scenarios:
• Sending case files to wrong email recipient
• Discussing cases in public places
• Leaving files in car or public transport
• Using unsecured public WiFi for work
• Sharing login credentials with colleagues
• Failing to lock laptop when away from desk
Immediate Actions (Within 1 Hour):
  1. Stop the breach if possible (e.g., recall email, secure physical documents)
  2. Notify your line manager and Data Protection Officer immediately
  3. Document exactly what happened, when, and what data was involved
  4. Do not attempt to cover up or delay reporting
Within 24 Hours:
  • DPO assesses severity and risk to data subjects
  • Contain the breach and prevent further data loss
  • Begin investigation into root cause
Within 72 Hours:
  • Report to ICO if risk to individuals' rights and freedoms
  • Notify affected individuals if high risk of harm
  • Document all actions taken
Disciplinary Consequences:
Minor breach (first offense): Written warning + retraining
Serious breach: Final written warning + suspension
Gross negligence: Immediate dismissal + potential legal action
Intentional breach: Dismissal + criminal prosecution
Clients and investigation subjects have the following rights:
  1. Right to be informed - Privacy notices explaining data use
  2. Right of access - Subject Access Request (SAR) within 1 month
  3. Right to rectification - Correct inaccurate personal data
  4. Right to erasure - "Right to be forgotten" (with exceptions)
  5. Right to restrict processing - Limit how data is used
  6. Right to data portability - Receive data in machine-readable format
  7. Right to object - Stop processing for direct marketing
  8. Rights related to automated decisions - Challenge AI/algorithmic decisions
How to Handle SAR:
Forward immediately to DPO → Do not delete anything → Respond within 30 days → Free of charge (unless excessive/repeat requests)
✅ Best Practices Checklist:
✓ Encrypt all case files before sending
✓ Use secure file transfer services (no personal email)
✓ Password-protect sensitive documents
✓ Anonymize data where possible
✓ Regular data audits and deletion of old files
✓ Annual data protection training mandatory

Professional Code of Conduct

Standards of behavior and ethical guidelines for investigators

📖 Professional Standards All investigators must maintain the highest ethical standards and comply with legal requirements. Our reputation depends on the integrity of every team member.

🎯 Core Values
  • Integrity: Honesty in all dealings with clients, subjects, and colleagues
  • Confidentiality: Never disclose case information without authorization
  • Objectivity: Remain impartial and report facts without bias
  • Professionalism: Maintain courteous, respectful conduct at all times
  • Competence: Only accept cases within your skill and knowledge level
✅ Expected Professional Behavior
  • Conduct all investigations within UK legal boundaries
  • Maintain objectivity - report findings truthfully even if unfavorable to client
  • Dress appropriately: Business attire for client meetings, discreet clothing for surveillance
  • Never misrepresent identity unless legally authorized (undercover operations)
  • Respect privacy rights - no trespassing or illegal entry
  • Maintain professional relationships with law enforcement and court officials
  • Complete thorough, accurate, and timely reports
  • Keep client informed of progress and any issues
❌ Strictly Prohibited Activities
🚫 Illegal surveillance (hacking, wiretapping, GPS tracking without consent)
🚫 Trespassing on private property
🚫 Impersonating law enforcement or government officials
🚫 Bribing witnesses or officials
🚫 Falsifying evidence or reports
🚫 Accepting gifts/payments from investigation subjects
🚫 Using intimidation or harassment tactics
🚫 Sharing case information on social media
What You CAN Do:
  • Observe and photograph people in public spaces
  • Review publicly available records and databases
  • Interview witnesses with their consent
  • Use open-source intelligence (OSINT) from social media
  • Conduct background checks with proper authorization
  • Follow subjects in public (maintaining reasonable distance)
What You CANNOT Do:
  • Enter private property without permission
  • Install tracking devices without consent
  • Intercept communications (calls, emails, texts)
  • Photograph through windows into private homes
  • Hack into digital accounts or devices
  • Use drones over private property without permission
  • Pretend to be someone else to obtain information (pretext calling)
⚠️ Gray Areas - Seek Approval:
• Using publicly available but sensitive information
• Surveillance near schools or places of worship
• Recording conversations (one-party consent in UK)
• Working with foreign jurisdictions
• Cases involving minors or vulnerable adults
Scenario 1: Personal Connection
A client asks you to investigate someone you know socially.
Action: Immediately disclose to supervisor. Case will be reassigned.

Scenario 2: Competing Clients
Two clients hire you for cases that may be connected or conflicting.
Action: Disclose to both clients. Obtain written consent or decline one case.

Scenario 3: Financial Interest
You discover information that could financially benefit you personally.
Action: Report immediately. Do not act on information. Recuse yourself.

Scenario 4: Romantic Relationship
You develop feelings for someone connected to your investigation.
Action: Report immediately. Case will be reassigned. Relationships with subjects/clients prohibited during and after investigation.
💡 Professional Development
• Annual ethics training mandatory for all investigators
• Quarterly case review meetings to discuss ethical dilemmas
• Access to legal counsel for guidance on complex cases
• Mentorship program for new investigators
📞 Ethics Hotline
Confidential reporting for ethical concerns: 0800-ETHICS-01 | ethics@snoopandpry.co.uk
Anonymous reporting available via external service

Health & Safety Policy

Comprehensive workplace safety and field operation protocols

📖 Purpose To ensure the safety and wellbeing of all employees, particularly those conducting field investigations which can involve unpredictable and potentially hazardous situations.

🎯 Risk Assessment Required Before:
  • Surveillance in high-crime areas
  • Following potentially violent subjects
  • Night-time operations
  • Operations involving confrontation
  • International investigations
  • Working alone in remote locations
Risk Levels:
🟢 Low: Standard office work, records research
🟡 Medium: Daytime surveillance in public areas
🟠 High: Surveillance of potentially dangerous individuals
🔴 Critical: Immediate threat to safety - requires police involvement
✅ Field Safety Protocols
  • Check-in system: Text location every 2 hours to operations center
  • Panic button: All field staff issued personal safety alarms
  • Buddy system: High-risk cases require two-person teams
  • Emergency contacts: Memorize and have written backup
  • First aid kit: Mandatory in all field vehicles
  • Vehicle maintenance: Weekly safety checks documented
  • Weather monitoring: Delay operations in severe conditions
  • Escape routes: Always identify before starting surveillance
🚨 Emergency Protocols
If threatened or in danger:
1. Abort the investigation immediately - your safety comes first
2. Move to a safe, public location
3. Call 999 if immediate danger
4. Call operations center: 0800-OPS-CENTER
5. Do NOT engage physically - retreat and report

If you witness a crime:
• Call police immediately
• Do not intervene unless life-threatening emergency
• Document what you saw from safe distance
• Preserve evidence but do not contaminate scene
• Provide statement to police as witness
Pre-Operation Vehicle Check:
  • Fuel level (minimum half tank for surveillance)
  • Tire pressure and tread depth
  • All lights functioning
  • Windscreen clear and wipers working
  • Emergency kit present (first aid, flashlight, blanket)
  • Fire extinguisher accessible
While Operating:
  • No mobile phone use while driving (hands-free only)
  • Follow all traffic laws - never exceed speed limit
  • Maintain safe distance from subject (3-4 cars)
  • If subject drives recklessly, break off pursuit
  • Regular breaks every 2 hours on long assignments
  • Report all accidents immediately, regardless of fault
Accident Procedure:
  1. Ensure safety of all parties
  2. Call emergency services if injuries
  3. Exchange details with other drivers
  4. Photograph damage and scene
  5. Call company insurance line within 24 hours
  6. Complete accident report form
Investigation work can be mentally demanding. The company provides:

Support Services:
  • Employee Assistance Program (EAP) - 24/7 confidential counseling
  • Quarterly wellbeing checks with occupational health
  • Stress management training
  • Peer support network
  • Mental health first aiders in each office
Warning Signs of Burnout:
  • Persistent fatigue and sleep problems
  • Increased cynicism or detachment
  • Reduced performance and concentration
  • Physical symptoms (headaches, tension)
  • Emotional exhaustion
✅ Self-Care Best Practices:
• Take your full lunch breaks
• Use annual leave regularly
• Maintain work-life boundaries
• Debrief after difficult cases
• Talk to supervisor if workload overwhelming
📞 EAP Helpline: 0800-WELLBEING | Available 24/7, completely confidential
📋 Office Safety
  • Ergonomic workstation assessments on request
  • Regular screen breaks (5 min every hour)
  • Fire drills quarterly - know your evacuation route
  • First aiders on every floor
  • Report hazards immediately via safety@snoopandpry.co.uk

IT & Email Communications Policy

Acceptable use of company devices and digital security protocols

📖 Purpose To protect company and client data through secure use of technology systems while enabling efficient operations.

🎯 Acceptable Use Policy Company devices and systems are primarily for business purposes. Limited personal use permitted during breaks (checking personal email, brief calls) provided it doesn't interfere with work.

✅ Mandatory Security Requirements
  • Passwords: Minimum 12 characters, mix of upper/lowercase, numbers, symbols
  • Password changes: Every 90 days
  • Two-factor authentication (2FA): Enabled on all accounts
  • Screen lock: Automatic after 5 minutes of inactivity
  • VPN: Mandatory for all remote access
  • Software updates: Install immediately when prompted
  • Antivirus: Must be active and up-to-date
  • Encryption: Full disk encryption on all laptops
⚠️ Phishing Warning Signs:
• Unexpected attachments or links
• Urgent requests for passwords or money transfers
• Spelling/grammar errors in "official" emails
• Sender email doesn't match company domain
• Requests to bypass normal procedures

If suspicious: Do NOT click. Forward to itsecurity@snoopandpry.co.uk
📧 Email Best Practices
  • Use company email for all work communications
  • Check "To" and "CC" fields before sending
  • Encrypt sensitive attachments (IT provides tools)
  • Use clear, professional subject lines
  • Include confidentiality footer (auto-added)
  • Don't include sensitive info in subject lines
  • Archive or delete old emails regularly
Good Password Strategy:
Use a passphrase instead of password. Example:

❌ Weak: password123
❌ Better but still weak: P@ssw0rd!
✅ Strong: Blue$Elephant#Dances42!

Password Manager:
Company provides LastPass Enterprise for secure password storage.
• Never reuse passwords across sites
• Let password manager generate random passwords
• Master password must be memorized (not written down)

Never:
• Share passwords with anyone (including IT unless they request password reset)
• Write passwords on sticky notes
• Store in unencrypted files
• Use personal information (birthdays, names, etc.)
Home Office Requirements:
  • Secure WiFi with WPA3 encryption
  • Separate work area with privacy
  • No family members accessing work devices
  • Laptop locked when away from desk
  • Video call backgrounds show no sensitive info
Public WiFi - Strictly Prohibited:
Never connect to work systems using:
• Coffee shop WiFi
• Hotel WiFi
• Airport WiFi
• Any public network

If you must work remotely: Use mobile hotspot or VPN only

Lost/Stolen Device Protocol:
1. Call IT Security immediately: 0800-IT-URGENT
2. We will remotely wipe the device
3. File police report
4. Complete incident report form
5. Change all passwords

Device tracking is enabled on all company devices
❌ Prohibited Activities
  • Downloading unauthorized software or browser extensions
  • Visiting inappropriate, illegal, or malicious websites
  • Sharing confidential information via personal email/cloud
  • Using USB drives without encryption and approval
  • Connecting personal devices to company network
  • Using company resources for personal business ventures
  • Cryptocurrency mining or illegal file sharing

Social Media & Public Communications

Guidelines for online presence and maintaining client discretion

📖 Policy Statement Employees must not disclose any information about ongoing investigations, clients, or company operations on social media or public forums.

🎯 Core Guidelines
  • Never post about cases, clients, or investigation methods
  • Don't identify yourself as investigator if compromises covert work
  • Company-related posts require management pre-approval
  • Maintain professional image - inappropriate posts reflect on company
  • Block attempts to solicit case information
  • Report suspected social engineering attempts
  • Privacy settings should be maximum on personal accounts
  • Assume anything posted online is permanent and public
Real Incident Examples:
• Investigator posted photo from surveillance location - subject recognized area
• LinkedIn profile revealed covert investigator identity to subject
• Facebook check-in compromised undercover operation
• Tweet about "frustrating case" led client to question competence
Think Before You Post:
❓ Could this reveal information about a case?
❓ Could this be traced back to the company?
❓ Would I be comfortable with this being shown in court?
❓ Does this reflect professional standards?

If any answer is "no" - don't post it!
✅ Acceptable Social Media Use
  • Sharing company press releases (with permission)
  • General industry news and trends
  • Professional development achievements
  • Company social events (pre-approved photos)
📞 Social Media Incident Reporting
If you see company/case info posted online: communications@snoopandpry.co.uk

Avoiding situations that compromise professional judgment

📖 Definition A conflict of interest occurs when personal interests could interfere with professional duties or create appearance of impropriety.

🎯 Common Conflict Scenarios

✅ Disclosure Requirements Report potential conflicts within 24 hours to your supervisor and compliance officer. Include:
• Nature of the conflict
• Parties involved
• Potential impact on investigation
• Proposed resolution

When in Doubt, Disclose
It's better to report a potential conflict that turns out to be acceptable than to hide one that later causes problems. All disclosures are handled confidentially.

Disciplinary & Grievance Procedures

Fair process for addressing misconduct and employee concerns

📖 Progressive Discipline Most issues addressed through escalating warnings before termination:
1. Verbal warning (recorded, stays on file 6 months)
2. Written warning (stays on file 12 months)
3. Final written warning (stays on file 18 months)
4. Dismissal

⚠️ Gross Misconduct (Immediate Dismissal)
• Theft, fraud, or dishonesty
• Breach of client confidentiality
• Illegal activities during investigations
• Violence, threats, or harassment
• Under influence of drugs/alcohol on duty
• Serious negligence causing harm
• Deliberate damage to property
• Falsifying timesheets or expenses

Process: Immediate suspension → Investigation → Disciplinary hearing → Decision
✅ Your Rights in Disciplinary Process
  • Written notice of allegations
  • Reasonable time to prepare defense
  • Right to be accompanied (colleague or union rep)
  • Present your case and call witnesses
  • Written outcome with reasons
  • Right to appeal
📝 Raising a Grievance If you have concerns about:
• Treatment by colleagues or managers
• Working conditions
• Bullying or harassment
• Discrimination
• Health and safety

Process:
1. Informal discussion with manager (if appropriate)
2. Formal written grievance to HR
3. Investigation by impartial party
4. Grievance hearing
5. Written outcome
6. Right to appeal

Confidentiality: All disciplinary and grievance matters handled with strict confidentiality. Only those directly involved will know details.
